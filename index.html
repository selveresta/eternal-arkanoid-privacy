Privacy Policy — Eternal Arkanoid

Effective date: August 19, 2026 · Developer: Evergreen Studio

This policy describes how the mobile game Eternal Arkanoid (package com.evergreenstudio.arkanoid, the “Game”) handles data.

Data we collect ourselves

None. The Game has no account system and no servers of its own. Your progress (levels, coins, best scores, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

Third-party services

The Game integrates the following Google services, which may process data as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded ads. AdMob may process your advertising ID, IP address, and device information to serve and measure ads. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent dialog is shown before any ad personalisation, and you can change your choice at any time via Pause → Privacy Options in the Game. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data practices.
  • Google Play Games Services — optional sign-in used for cloud backup of your progress and for leaderboards. Google processes your Play Games profile according to the Google Privacy Policy. If sign-in fails or is declined, the Game continues fully offline.

Ads and purchases

All ads are optional rewarded ads that you explicitly choose to watch. The Game contains no in-app purchases.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving is configured through Google’s consent framework in accordance with applicable law.

Data deletion

Local progress can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Cloud saves can be removed via your Google account’s data controls (Play Games data).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
