Privacy Policy — Eternal Arkanoid

Effective date: September 21, 2026 · Developer: Evergreen Studio

This policy describes how the mobile game Eternal Arkanoid (package com.evergreenstudio.arkanoid, the “Game”) handles data.

Data we collect ourselves

None. The Game has no account system and no servers of its own. Your progress (levels, coins, best scores, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The one feature that connects two devices — online duels, described below — runs on Unity’s services, not ours, and involves no name, account, chat or voice.

Third-party services

The Game integrates the following third-party services, which may process data as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded ads. AdMob may process your advertising ID, IP address, and device information to serve and measure ads. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent dialog is shown before any ad personalisation, and you can change your choice at any time via Pause → Privacy Options in the Game. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data practices.
  • Google Play Games Services — optional sign-in used for cloud backup of your progress and for leaderboards. Google processes your Play Games profile according to the Google Privacy Policy. If sign-in fails or is declined, the Game continues fully offline.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Multiplayer Services, including Relay) — used only for online duels, and only when you choose Play online. To find an opponent and connect the two devices, the Game signs in anonymously: Unity creates a random player ID for this installation, which is not linked to your name, email or Google account. While you search and play, Unity processes that ID, your IP address and a temporary match session (a small public room that exists only until the match ends), and relays the match data between the two devices over an encrypted connection. Your opponent sees only your paddle moving and the score: no name, ID, location or any other information about you is shown to them, and there is no chat. The Game does not store the match data. Unity acts as our processor; see the Unity Privacy Policy for how it handles this data and for how long. If nobody is found, or you are offline, you play against a computer-controlled opponent and nothing is sent.

Ads and purchases

All ads are optional rewarded ads that you explicitly choose to watch. The Game contains no in-app purchases.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving is configured through Google’s consent framework in accordance with applicable law.

Data deletion

Local progress can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it, which also removes the anonymous online player ID from your device. Cloud saves can be removed via your Google account’s data controls (Play Games data). We cannot link the anonymous online ID back to you; if you want Unity’s copy of it deleted, write to the address below and we will pass the request to Unity where it can be actioned.
